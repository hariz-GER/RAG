HR Policy Handbook

1. Working Hours
Employees are expected to work from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday. A one-hour lunch break may be taken between 12:30 PM and 2:30 PM, based on team requirements.

2. Attendance
Employees should mark attendance every working day. If an employee is unable to work due to illness, emergency, or personal reasons, they must inform their reporting manager as early as possible.

3. Leave Policy
Employees are eligible for paid leave, sick leave, and public holidays according to company policy. Planned leave should be requested at least five working days in advance. Emergency leave may be approved by the reporting manager.

4. Remote Work
Remote work may be allowed with manager approval. Employees working remotely must be available during core working hours and should attend scheduled meetings on time.

5. Code of Conduct
Employees must behave respectfully with colleagues, clients, and partners. Harassment, discrimination, bullying, or offensive behavior will not be tolerated.

6. Data Security
Employees must protect company data, customer information, passwords, and confidential documents. Sensitive information must not be shared through personal email, public links, or unauthorized applications.

7. Use of Company Assets
Company laptops, software, accounts, and devices must be used responsibly and primarily for work purposes. Any damage, loss, or security issue must be reported immediately.

8. Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted periodically to evaluate goals, responsibilities, collaboration, and growth. Managers will provide feedback and discuss improvement plans where needed.

9. Resignation and Notice Period
Employees who resign must serve the notice period mentioned in their employment agreement. During the notice period, employees are expected to complete handover activities and return company assets.

10. Policy Updates
The company may update HR policies from time to time. Employees will be notified when important policy changes are made.
